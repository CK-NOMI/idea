## T/H: block anchor residual as new formal target, not original dense model
For shallow causal states x_t, block boundary a known from committed history, compute r_a=D(x_<=a)-x_a (deep anchor); define p_t=softmax(W(x_t+gamma_{t-a} r_a)), q_t=softmax(W(x_t+gamma_{t-a} A_e(x_a))). Shallow x must not depend on unknown r_a (late fusion). No standalone draft LM. Snapshot fixed within rollout. At anchor gamma_0=1, later scalar gamma learned on training prefixes. One-layer suffix can compute exact anchor query with K/V projected from shallow states, retaining all earlier shallow K/V. For multi-layer dense suffix this is NOT exact: previous skipped deeper states affect K/V; must explicitly redesign/train a new anchor-only or shallow-memory cross-attention suffix and include all cache costs, or recompute dense states (which may erase benefit). No claim original teacher sequence equality.
Exact same-new-p sampling in real arithmetic: anchor depends only on committed prefix, so compute/prefetch independently of speculative candidates. Generate B sequential q candidates and retain their actual q. For each reached position accept with min(1,p(X)/q(X)), reject and draw positive residual normalized by TV; after first reject discard remaining proposed suffix, then directly AR from actual p for remaining positions. Conditional output mass min(p,q)+(p-q)_+=p; causal induction proves full fixed-horizon sequence distribution; greedy compare proposed token with true p argmax using fixed tie rule, correct and discard suffix on mismatch. Temperature/top-p require applied actual normalized p,q; EOS and production finite precision not tested. No bonus because fixed anchor boundary. A rejected token changes subsequent shallow states; recompute only invalidated positions, but anchor stays valid within block. Simultaneous resources necessary for overlap. Speculation after anchor arrives early is optional and a tuned partial-block/pipelined baseline is necessary. Same-p anchor AR directly uses r_a and avoids speculative extra head/replay cost.
Quality: for teacher logits l and anchor logits l', KL(softmax(l)||softmax(l'))=log E_p exp(delta)-E_p delta with delta=l'-l, hence <=(max delta-min delta)^2/8 by Hoeffding; classical bound, not proof real errors small. Counterexample d_a=0, later teacher deep residual shifts probability on a critical token: anchor model cannot repair it; rare-task/long-context accuracy may fail despite low average NLL.
## P/T: overlap model (not wall time)
Let a0 be per-token shallow + direct head latency, D be exposed deep anchor latency, L be block verification extra latency incl projection/transfer. Same-anchor serial AR D+B*a0. Candidate max(D,B*a0)+L+a0*E[R], where R=B-j if first rejection at position j (1-based); P proxy E[R]/B = sum_{i=1}^{B-1}(1-product_{j=1}^i overlap_j)/B on fixed teacher prefixes, NOT actual free-running expectation. Actual fallback head/caches may cost more. S <=2 even with zero overhead because sum/max <=2. At balance D=B*a0 and L/(B*a0)=.08, observed proxy suffix ratios .041820/.082078/.167133 give 1.782817/1.721055/1.603678x at B2/4/8 vs SAME-ANCHOR SERIAL AR only; no speed claim vs dense SD or ordinary tuned pipeline. At B4, for 1.5x, with r=.082078, h=.08 and d=D/(Ba0), require 1.5*(max(d,1)+h+r)<=d+1, so d between .743117 and 1.513766; at 2x impossible with positive overhead. End-to-end train+rollout with inference fraction f and overhead h_total: 1/[1-f(1-1/S)+h_total]. At S=1.721055, f=.7,h_total=.03 gives ~1.36x; not 1.5x. Separate devices, compute resource fairness, RTT, memory bandwidth, verification head cost, and physical overlap must be measured.
