## Hypothesis T/H
Frozen bank B of authorized public-prompt trajectories from other clients/previous versions defines empirical conditional r_B(x|h)=N_B(hx)/N_B(h), with native MTP q0 fallback at empty nodes. Define actual per-step mixture q=(1-alpha)q0+alpha r_B. Independently draw proposal per step from this conditional; do not choose an entire source and mistakenly use per-step mixture (posterior source weights then differ). At fixed policy p, acceptance min(1,p/q) and normalized positive residual recover p; after rejection use p continuation or exact repeated SD. Conditional on frozen B and independent RNG per sample, joint output across clients factorizes into their current policies; sharing B alone does not introduce sample correlation. Bank selection must not depend on current sample RNG/reward; any selected old successes still require actual empirical q; no old logits needed for empirical proposal. Requires same authorized public prompt/tokenizer/version contract; private responses must not be exported. No guarantee of full numerical equality, EOS/top-p/runtime. This produces on-policy samples; it does NOT reproduce PubSwap's off-policy learning benefit. For fixed p, expected ordinary policy gradient unchanged; G2 needs a further independent causal learning benefit, not mere speedup. Same grouped-flat can reproduce bank and routing exactly.
