## New hypothesis H: cost-aware valid-output equivalence classes
Consider RLVR where multiple trajectories y in equivalence class C(x) pass the same external task verifier. A formal target may be retrained to favor lower predicted verified generation cost, constrained by task correctness, length, and a KL trust region from a frozen reference. Exact SD only preserves each newly fixed target p, not the original target distribution. Conditional ideal objective min_p E_p[c(y)] + tau KL(p||p0), supported on C, solution p*(y) proportional p0(y) exp(-c(y)/tau). Assumes known static trajectory costs, support and valid class; classical Gibbs tilt, no new theorem. Learned costs depend on policy and actual MTP, not free. Need compare ordinary canonicalization/length penalty/quality-matched RL and identical delegated flat, not just AR.
