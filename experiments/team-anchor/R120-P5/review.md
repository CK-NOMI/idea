## Decision
Reject short vocabulary head, simple full projection ridge and ordinary regional sufficient-statistic grouping as main mechanism under these tested conditions. Retain correct verifier and oracle budget as infrastructure, lookup as measured baseline. Do not interpret 1.33x lookup as novel or compare oracle against it across different inputs. Stop incremental tuning. New research must start from a substantive HFL-specific resource/learning problem and a pretrained endogenous proposal (not train another weak head on 24 docs); show benefit vs identical delegated flat under fixed network/information budget, SD verification feedback affecting hierarchy decisions, quality-matched same-dtype strong SD and lookup. No ready new mechanism or new code to ask Codex to execute; require literature/duplicate review and independent CPU screening before GPU.
