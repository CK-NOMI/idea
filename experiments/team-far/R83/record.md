## T1 exact matrix and gradient
At fixed causal frontier with independent drafts and R81 fixed order/m uniform output-independent flags, f_i(j)=F_i(j/m), j=0..m, f_0(j)=j/m. For each positive p_v,q_v, x=j p_v/q_v; when x>=m add q_v to M_i[j,m], otherwise k=floor(x), a=x-k, add q_v(1-a) at [j,k] and q_v a at [j,k+1]; row0 set zero (since f(0)=0). Then f_i=M_i f_(i-1) exactly by R81 interpolation, S=e_m^T M_N...M_1 f_0. Matrices nonnegative, rows j>=1 sum1; product associative, not commutative; consecutive region products can be aggregated on balanced tree. This is deterministic probability evaluation for learning, not R82 realized random-state maps for execution. Both need fixed same frontier. Dense matrix reduction O(N m^3) work, span O(log N) matrix multiplies; serial vector O(NmV) direct recurrence can be cheaper. M construction O(NmV), reverse local O(mV) given f and adjoint. Can use two-sided scans or tree product/adjoint. F_i real arithmetic piecewise smooth; ties choose one-sided branch (not universal gradient), q=0 handling/top-p/finite precision not implemented; positive softmax in experiment. R81 correctness inherited only in restricted fixed frontier, not new full multistep/greedy proof.
L=-log(S)/N; dL/dM_i=lambda_i f_(i-1)^T with lambda_N=-e_m/(NS), lambda_(i-1)=M_i^T lambda_i. For x<m, local contribution q_v f_k+(j p_v-k q_v)(f_(k+1)-f_k), hence partials d/dp=j(f_(k+1)-f_k), d/dq=f_k-k(f_(k+1)-f_k). For x>=m derivatives 0,f_m. Sum weighted by lambda_i[j], then softmax chain rule for p/q. No MC gradient variance for fixed p/q cohort; sample contexts still stochastic, -log per-cohort changes weighting vs expected raw repair and can be ill-conditioned S->0, not universal variance reduction. m=1 exactly mean -log(1-TV_i); strong additive baseline. Ordinary shared p and q parameter requires both chain contributions, stop-gradient is an alternative objective. Directly adapting p to reduce repair can change formal model quality, so cannot claim original objective preserved by joint penalty. Equal parameters for CE-only p across draft arms; joint penalty changes formal objective. 
## T2 independence and hierarchical sufficient statistics
If lane contexts independently drawn, M_i random (p/q local), E[M_N...M_1]=E[M_N]...E[M_1] by finite entrywise expansion/independence. Within each fixed slot, clients can aggregate mean M_i (weighted by sampling law), and cloud use product means to get expected S exactly. For contiguous groups of correlated contexts internally, group must average full ordered product instead; independent groups factor. For arbitrary correlated contexts factorization false. For -log E S, can differentiate means under finite fixed sampling law; equals neither E[-log S] nor mean local log; Jensen and coupling differences. Each region needs forward and adjoint vectors, per-slot local Jacobian not transferred. Exact mean matrices and tree sufficient statistics are not an FL optimization convergence theorem; multiple local SGD with different versions does not equal common-snapshot gradient. Same-info delegated flat can reproduce every step, classical distributed compositional optimization and prefix scans are strong prior art.
