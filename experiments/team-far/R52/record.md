## T: exact gradient of multi-token survival
Fixed legitimate starting prefix, snapshot, finite V, positive postprocessed softmax p_theta (complete single LM) and q_theta (its own early exit), K=2. Let m_i=min(p_i,q_i), a_i=sum_j min(p(j|hi),q(j|hi)), A=sum_i m_i(1+a_i). Both p and q can depend on the same theta. Work at differentiability points (ties need chosen directional treatment). Let d_i=grad m_i, g_i=grad a_i, D=sum d_i; freeze proposal probability q_i at the sampling snapshot for importance weighting/stop-gradient; take X~q. T_X=d_X/q_X, S_X=(a_X d_X+m_X g_X)/q_X. E[T]=D, E[S]=grad A-D. Raw estimator T+S is unbiased. Conditional root estimator D+S is unbiased. For any scalar b fixed before the draw, H_b=D+S-b(T-D) is unbiased, and H_b has conditional trace variance Var(S)+b^2 Var(T)-2b Cov_trace(S,T). If Var(T)>0, optimal b=E[(S-ES)·(T-D)]/E||T-D||². Var(T)=0 implies no scalar CV gain. Do not differentiate b or sampling density as if ordinary autodiff of a moving importance law; gradient target is derivative of A under fixed snapshot. Standard SD verification supplies p along proposed path, including first child even when first token later rejected if full block actually verified; do not assume these activations are preserved or free for learning. If only committed prefixes retained, cannot apply without conditional selection correction. No need independent draft model; q shallow, p full, same output bias in experiment. One scalar VJP of weighted root logits suffices for D and sampled correction; full network backprop not tested. Ordinary teacher forcing can acquire same legal information.
General finite K: V_0(h)=0, V_k(h)=sum_i m_i(h)[1+V_(k-1)(hi)]. At any fixed positive sampling distribution r independent of differentiation and frozen b_i(h), recursively define Vhat_k=sum_i m_i(1+b_i)+m_X/r_X*(Vhat_(k-1)(hX)-b_X) with X~r. Induction gives E Vhat_k=V_k; finite support/window allows differentiating fixed law to obtain unbiased gradient away from ties. This is a recursive control-variate estimator, not guaranteed variance reduction for arbitrary learned baseline; K>2 untested. Actual proposal law can be q at anchor, then stopped for differentiation; if using old q, exact density and support needed. Sampling-only learning of EAL is not a substitute for formal NLL. Standard lossless SD kernel still needs actual q and p (min(1,p/q), residual, causal truncation, bonus/EOS); formal target is current p, not older p. Production greedy, temperature/top-p/finite precision not executed.

## T: population hierarchy gain versus calibration error
For region e, with workload weights absorbed into expectation, D_e=E_e||T-D||² and N_e=E_e[(T-D)·S], b_e*=N_e/D_e for D_e>0. Regional expected conditional trace variance R_e(b)=R_e(b_e*)+D_e(b-b_e*)². Global b*=sum N_e/sum D_e; oracle global-to-regional reduction exactly sum_e D_e(b_e*-b*)². For independent calibration estimate b_hat_e, expected excess test variance relative to oracle is sum D_e E[(b_hat_e-b_e*)²]. Comparing learned regional vs learned global requires regional oracle separation exceeding differential estimation error, not merely larger non-IID drift. Same formula within clients and groups; weighted moments are additive across HFL. All-zero D has no opportunity. In a fixed anchor with calibration context known, E[S·(T-D)]=N; denominator exact from root probabilities/derivative. One sampled successor provides numerator estimate. Subtract prior b0*T from S, estimate b_hat=b0+sum((S-b0 T)·(T-D))/sum D, unbiased conditional on fixed calibration contexts and fixed b0, since E[T·(T-D)]=D. Ratio with random contexts estimates empirical weighted oracle, not necessarily population unbiased. b0=.8 in test. Heuristic b=mean sampled child overlap is strong and nearly as good, no vector gradient moment required. Region/Cloud simply sum 2 moments and counts; same-information flat precisely reproduces. Cannot claim privacy, nonlinear local SGD equivalence or actual advantage.
Cost: condition on contexts, if raw/controlled variances v0/v1, iid averaged auxiliary gradient MSE at n samples is v/n; total task+auxiliary variance includes between-context CE and EAL gradient variance and cross-covariance. Therefore conditional reduction cannot be converted directly to equal-quality time savings. Full root+one child vs complete K2 tree of V child nodes is a count proxy, not FLOPs/latency; calibration and holding activations, VJP/communication and optimizer costs must be included. Strong baseline ordinary direct training plus scalar CV, single global coefficient, full exact EAL (small V), WTV/VSD/VAT, same-info flat. No true physical bottleneck found.
