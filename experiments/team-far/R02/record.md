## Problem
Online federated policy learning with expensive local reward/label evaluation (e.g. private test suites or expert assessments); independent target-policy trajectories from a fixed snapshot. Rejected speculative positions already evaluated in a production block verifier provide a candidate extra feature Z; actual final target output is Y. No independent draft LLM: internal shallow/MTP head q. Regional nodes train private reward residual predictors and allocate query probabilities, cloud aggregates corrected formal policy gradients. Objective is fixed full-reward expected return, not draft acceptance, not GRPO clipping or pairwise DPO. Optimize same-quality total time and oracle-query budget. ## T
Let S=gradient log P_theta(Y), R local expensive reward with finite second moment, A=all cheap pre-query features (including Y or its available features, p/q version, Z), m(A) predicted reward detached; choose pi(A)>0 before observing R, draw I independently conditional on A,R with Pr(I=1|A,R)=pi(A). Estimator G=[m(A)+I/pi(A)*(R-m(A))]S. Conditioning on all potential outcomes and S proves E_I G=R S; no requirement that rejected tokens themselves are valid training labels. For fixed predictor, conditional selection noise trace is (1/pi-1)(R-m)^2||S||^2. Additional variance after averaging equals E[(1/pi-1)a(A)] with a(A)=E[(R-m)^2||S||^2|A]. Independent trajectories/query randomness, fixed snapshot, valid reward semantics; no unbiasedness claim for self-normalized GRPO, AdamW paths or adaptive selection without conditioning.
For expected query-cost budget E[c(A)pi(A)]<=B, minimize E[a(A)/pi(A)] subject to pi_min<=pi<=1. KKT gives pi*=clip(sqrt(a/(lambda c)),pi_min,1), lambda chosen by monotone budget solve. For region e, weights w_e enter squared aggregate coefficients under independent contributions; exact finite allocation needs only per-region monotone budget curves, not raw reward labels, but ordinary active sampling can also do this. Query counts and cost estimates are not measured times.
Analytic illustration only: equal two strata a=.81,.01, c=1, average pi=.25, no clipping. Uniform extra variance 1.23; optimal pi=.45,.05 gives .59 (52.03% reduction in *additional query variance*, not total gradient variance, cost or actual convergence). If a equal no improvement. If Z carries no conditional incremental information or baseline has same information at equal cost, advantage vanishes. If reward oracle independent of verifier randomness given full prompt and final Y, conditional Bayes reward prediction cannot be improved merely by Z; any advantage must be computational (cheap already-computed representations/features), not new label information. No actual model correlation, learning, latency, or label acquisition measured. Strong baseline AR can evaluate q/branches, and same-info flat can execute same allocation; no guaranteed G2.
SD target: exact standard acceptance using actual postprocessed p and q, positive residual, causal truncation and correct bonus/EOS, independent trajectory RNG; greedy target argmax. Query choices occur after completion, no changes to generated output. Full production numerics/greedy not tested.
