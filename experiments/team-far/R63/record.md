## T: genuine information and cost distinction
Fixed snapshot, private prompt/reward/task, potential target trajectory Y~p, formal local gradient G=R(Y) grad log p(Y), no group normalization or PPO multi-step, exogenous client weights. Let H be committed prefix, X be fresh shallow proposal path from same model q, before verifying. Let V be the committed block prefix (variable length, includes correction or bonus), F=(H,X,V,other completed-block trace). Assume after completed block all future random numbers are independent and formal conditional suffix kernel depends only on V and fixed policy/task; G is a function only of full formal trajectory and fixed task. Then E[G|F]=E[G|V]. Rejected past candidates or acceptance bits cannot provide extra oracle prediction power conditional on full committed prefix under these assumptions. This is a sufficiency statement, not a claim that extra deterministic features cannot help a restricted learned predictor or computation cost. No claim for unresolved block, hidden environment state, adaptive model changes, noncausal reward or reuse of dependent random seeds.

Before verification, X is coupled to future Y, so m_H=E[G|H], m_X=E[G|H,X]; E||G-m_H||²-E||G-m_X||²=E||m_X-m_H||² >=0 (orthogonal conditional projection). Let m_V=E[G|V], then E[m_V|H,X]=m_X, hence explained variance from m_H <= m_X <= m_V (in trace; nested full filtration even if V alone does not reveal X). Strict examples p=q with one binary draft and terminal reward depending on it; no gain if G constant or m_X=m_H. Independent AR lookahead sampled from q but not coupled to actual formal future has E[G|H,X]=E[G|H]. A target-first reverse-coupled AR trace from R31 can reproduce the joint law after evaluating target; an AR decoder using the same cheap proposal and maximal-coupled conditional next token can also implement this information effect (essentially one-token speculation). Multi-token parallel verification is a physical advantage only if timed. Same-info flat with regional delegation can exactly implement predictor pooling. No HFL<->SD necessity established; no reverse effect on draft acceptance/training actually evaluated. Do not treat conditioning identities as new fundamental probability theory.

For fixed h,x length k, a_j=min(1,p(x_j|h x_<j)/q(...)), s_j=prod_{l<=j}a_l. Standard SD correction at first reject j drawn from normalized positive residual r_j. Given X, the posterior mean m_X is sum over first rejection j of s_{j-1}(1-a_j) E_{Z~r_j}[m(h x_<j Z)] plus s_k E_{Z~p}[m(hxZ)] (bonus subject to remaining length). All m are formal complete-gradient conditional means; actual p evaluations and full gradient conditional means are not free, so expression is oracle and motivates learning an amortized predictor without target verification. Conditional law of completed audited trajectories given X is not p, and selected full trajectories can be biased even though original potential Y marginal is p. With predictable m and audit B~Bern(pi(H,X)>0) independent of as-yet-unrevealed suffix/verification RNG conditional on H,X, Ghat=m+B(G-m)/pi has target mean E[G]. Keep same X on audit; replacing X with fresh independent draft changes conditional expectation. User-facing exact output still must finish; fixed version, legal q, causal residual and bonus, EOS/temperature/top-p/precision production not tested. 

R62 common-U audit covariance formula retained. For fixed audit pi and potential cohort full gradients g_i, residual r_i=g_i-m_i, conditional audit variance of mean adds N^-2 sum_{i,j} (1/max(pi_i,pi_j)-1) r_i dot r_j (diagonal included). This provides empirical calibration from complete held-out cohorts, without true moments. Predictor risk at fixed state = irreducible conditional variance + E||m-m*||². Finite-sample expansion of states may lose more than oracle information gain. For a single uniform pi and same full cost T, compared with prefix-only, draft observation cost increment d>0 and extra explained variance Delta, ideal new variance V'=V-(1/pi-1)Delta. New cost C'=C+(1-pi)d (under deterministic equal-cost cohort common audit) beats old iff (1/pi-1)Delta > V (1-pi)d/[C+(1-pi)d]. With learned predictors use reduction of residual prediction risk, and cross-client terms; not a universal inequality for heterogeneous max or real GPU. The cost threshold and actual calibration, rather than more proxy score optimization, are important.
