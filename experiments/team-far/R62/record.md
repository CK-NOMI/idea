## T: formal problem
All precommitted clients use a fixed formal policy, local private terminal reward, one on-policy REINFORCE gradient G_i=R(Y_i) grad log p_theta(Y_i) with fixed exogenous weights and independent potential trajectories conditional on joint committed prefixes H. Each training-only trajectory may be randomized-stopped after a committed prefix. No claim that incomplete user-facing outputs have full sequence distribution; no direct extension to GRPO groups, PPO clipping or multi-local-step FedAvg. Standard inner self-SD uses q from same formal model's shallow path, min(1,p/q), positive residual, causal discard/bonus; for completed trajectories the potential full target law is p, but the observed selected completed trajectories generally have selection bias when pi depends on H. Exactness of fixed formal gradient expectation instead comes from correction. Greedy/production temperature/top-p/numerics not audited.

Predictable m_i(H), B_i with marginal pi_i>0 conditional on H and independent of future potential suffixes conditional on H. Ghat_i=m_i+B_i(G_i-m_i)/pi_i. E[Ghat_i|H]=mu_i=E[G_i|H]. Conditional covariance for independent potential suffixes: diagonal Sigma_i/pi_i+(1/pi_i-1)e_i e_i^T, e_i=mu_i-m_i. Off-diagonal (P(B_i=B_j=1|H)/(pi_i pi_j)-1)e_i e_j^T. Under common U~Unif(0,1), B_i=1{U<pi_i}, off-diagonal coefficient=1/max(pi_i,pi_j)-1. Exact conditional predictors remove all cross-client covariance induced by shared continuation, but not marginal variance inflation. With learned m, signed cross term can increase/decrease aggregate trace; cannot assume perfect cancellation. This is an augmented inverse-probability estimator and coupling application, not new fundamental AIPW theory. 
Condition on potential completion times t_i (finish if continued) and a_i (prefix finish), B=max_i a_i, t_i>=a_i. If U independent of potential times given H, for x>=B, P(max_i completion_i > x)=P(union_{i:t_i>x}{B_i=1})>=max_{i:t_i>x} pi_i, equality simultaneously for all x with common U. Integrate survival to show shared uniform minimizes expected max at fixed marginal probabilities among all couplings with same independence from futures. No claim for shared GPU/batched resource contention or adaptation based on latent futures. All-or-none common audit with equal pi is a strong baseline; AR can use same rule. 
For fixed deterministic t_i sorted ascending (after baseline B), exact predictor and positive variances v_i, optimum of conditional variance plus lambda expected max cost can set pi=1 for t_i<=B and pi nonincreasing in t_i: replacing earlier lower pi with a later max never raises cost and decreases variance. For the remaining distinct sorted completion times, C=B+sum_i delta_i*pi_i, delta_i=t_i-max(B,t_{i-1}); minimize sum v_i/pi_i+lambda sum delta_i*pi_i with monotone pi in [epsilon,1]. Adjacent violators merge neighboring blocks when v_left/delta_left < v_right/delta_right; block optimum pi=clip(sqrt(sum v/(lambda sum delta))). Zero-cost nodes can be raised to 1; tied completion times can be merged. Classical separable convex isotonic optimization; deterministic model only. With stochastic durations, mean-time order need not be optimal; actual experiment uses it as candidate generator and evaluates held-out potential costs. For equal pi, W0=variance of conditional mean of aggregated gradient, W1=expected conditional residual variance under independent clients and exact predictors, f=B/T; fixed-snapshot cost-variance product relative to full = (f+(1-f)pi)*(W0+W1/pi)/(W0+W1); improves iff W1/W0 < pi(1-f)/f for 0<f,pi<1 and W0>0. This is fixed-MSE asymptotic independent-batch sample-cost proxy, not time to train to quality; R61 showed those can disagree. 
HFL/SD coupling limitation: regional predictors trained using local trajectory history, shared regional/root continuation seed and probabilities can be disseminated. HFL can alter learning predictions and continuation scheduling, which changes actual SD work, but formal model-dependent SD improvements and topology necessity not demonstrated. A strong same-delegation flat and ordinary AR can reproduce the mechanism; user-facing exact generation must still complete. Predictor fitting, inference, cache, communication, reward and gradient cost, synchronization and tail contention must be counted.
