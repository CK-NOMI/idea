## T/P: normalized removable critical path
For serial exposed fraction f of tuned strong baseline, reduction s of that stage, overhead h relative to baseline, T'/T=1-f*s+h. To achieve S-fold speed, f*s>=1-1/S+h. At overhead .03, f=.4/.5/.6/.7/.8 requires stage reductions for 1.5x .9083/.7267/.6056/.5190/.4542; for 2x 1.325/1.06/.8833/.7571/.6625.
