## T/H
Same R110 fixed sparse formal target p: gate off p=q; gate on two deep layers attend only active ancestors. NOT original dense target. Choose K according to known causal gate at first parent BEFORE drawing proposal. At each position assign independent (Uq,Uaccept,Urepair) uniforms; proposal X~q, accept with min(1,p(X)/q(X)), otherwise sample normalized positive residual. Accepted marginal min(p,q), repair marginal (p-q)+, hence output p. Conditional induction for committed history and fresh position tapes gives sequence law in exact arithmetic, irrespective of legal predictable block policy. Uncommitted speculative tape draws are discarded/reused on actual prefix, not conditioned on lookahead; no bonus, EOS, top-p or production finite precision guarantee. Same tape coupling can match K=1 per-token output (not ordinary inverse-CDF AR with different coupling). 
Wire protocol: shared exact head W and norm/snapshot, active parents h only; deep target computes p, receiver reconstructs q from h, receives actual candidate ID and two uniform doubles, reports first rejected position and corrected token or all accepted. Inactive p=q accepts deterministically, no need to send. Both caches crop to committed parents/active subset. All prefill active rows sent once; decoding packet header 8 bytes, per-active-row metadata24 + 4d hidden bytes + optional 4V q-logit bytes; response8. No raw q probability quantization, no measured physical network; in-process sender/receiver serialization only, same trusted snapshot, no auth/transport/security claims. Ordinary split SD with identical head replica can reproduce this exactly; token-ID replay with remote shallow replica may send even fewer bytes at additional compute/memory; not executed. HFL-specific incremental improvement not proved. Scope of exactness current formal p only.
