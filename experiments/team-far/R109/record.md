## T/H
Define causal shallow h_i and q_i. Partition absolute positions into fixed groups of four, decide gate g from shallow features at first position in group, before generating token there. All positions in group inherit g. For active indices I, apply both deep causal blocks ONLY to h_I, attending to earlier active indices; use their logits for p_i. For nonactive indices p_i=q_i. This is a NEW formal model, not lossless to dense teacher. Gate fixed version and causal. Greedy standard verifies p argmax, ties consistent. Standard stochastic SD with actual p/q, min ratio and positive residual, discard suffix on rejection, proper bonus/EOS, gives per-prefix p and by induction sequence law in exact arithmetic. At inactive positions p=q by definition; no deep KV rows needed at those positions in the new model. Full KV and real rollout not implemented or tested; original dense quality not guaranteed. If position encoding in deeper layers is relative/rotary, preserve absolute positions; toy has learned input positional embeddings only. ## P: performance feasibility 
For same block committed output, ideal binary deep-invocation cost model fraction f of baseline cost incurred whenever any active deep row, all other cost unchanged, deep-active block cost identical to dense; ratio=1-f(1-u). With f=.60, u=.214193 (aligned block) yields ratio .528516, speed1.8921; tokenwise u=.533203 gives .719922, speed1.3890. Relative to tokenwise gate speed1.3622, not 1.89. To reach 1.5x vs dense SD under these assumptions require f>=.4242, 2x f>=.6363. These are hypothetical costs, not predicted measured performance. Actual draft/verify boundaries misalign, bonus/correction, batching, KV, q computations, quality, training cost and gate overhead matter. Batch occupancy for independent B sequences =1-(1-u)^B; at B4 about .618, B16 about .979; shared deep-weight reads cannot be assumed saved at large batch. Must test direct sparse AR, ordinary MoD+SD, globally learned block gate, periodic block gate, same active-rate and quality, aligned vs unaligned real SD, batch regimes. No empirical basis for lifecycle 1.5x yet.
