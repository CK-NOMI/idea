## Decision
Retain feature cache as standard engineering baseline. Reject last-verification tape reuse as standalone innovation in tested regime; no 15% vs strong baseline, no G2 proof. Historical innovation 8.14 unchanged. Prior maturity G1=7.8,G2=6.8,G3=9.1,G4=4.5,G5=5,G6=5.5,G7=7.5. Actual small CPU evidence may improve implementation readiness but not justify GPU-scale/end-to-end score inflation; G1/G2/G4 fail, G3/G7 partial, G5 unverified, G6 insufficient. Next require genuine 1B/8B LayerSkip GPU profile R104, freeze depth sweep against full-finetune and standard cached training, fixed quality and budget, actual regional personalization, independently trained seeds, three nonIID/latency settings, physical network & full cost, 15% vs strong tuned baseline and CI. No claim of 80% success. Do not continue optimizing repeated prefix cache alone.
