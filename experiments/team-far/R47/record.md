## T: remove ratio estimation for the update sign
Fixed reference p0,q0 with strictly positive probabilities, fixed finite K, workload distribution and candidate theta independent of audit samples. m0(v)=product min(p0,q0) for a nonempty continuation v; mtheta analogous. Define H=E_h sum_v m0(v) log[mtheta(v)/m0(v)], A0=E_h sum_v m0(v)>0. R46 S=H/A0, log(A_theta/A0)>=H/A0. To certify nondecrease, estimate H directly; H>=0 is sufficient and does not require estimating a ratio. For an old full draft sequence X~q0, independent old verification uniforms, let I_j indicate acceptance of ALL first j proposals; do not include bonus or correction tokens. l_t=log[min(p_theta,q_theta)(X_t|hX_<t)/min(p0,q0)(X_t|hX_<t)], f_j=sum_{t<=j}l_t. Hard statistic Z=sum_j I_j f_j; E Z=H by marginal path acceptance. E[Z|X]=sum_j (product_{t<=j}alpha_t) f_j, alpha_t=min(1,p0(X_t)/q0(X_t)); this removes Bernoulli noise. Full block must actually be available even on early rejection; if production skips suffix distributions, conditional estimator requires extra evaluations. EOS/truncation and zero support need separate handling. No unbiased ratio claim. Samples from different model versions/behavior distributions cannot be merged naively. Candidate-dependent adaptive reuse of audit trajectories invalidates fixed-candidate confidence unless fresh holdout or simultaneous bound.

K=2 terminal Rao-Blackwell: draw root X from old q0. m_t denotes min of old p,q at step t, r1=l1(X), r2(X,y)=r1+l2(X,y); alpha1=m1(X)/q0(X). T(X)=alpha1[r1+sum_y m2(y|X)r2(X,y)]. Conditional on X, integrates second token and both Bernoulli uniforms; E T=H, Var(T)<=Var(soft)<=Var(hard) by total variance for nested sigma fields, conditional on start prefix. Only needs root and sampled child full rows, not all V child prefixes, when logits available. With root exact expectation R=sum_x m1(x)l1(x), define root control variate Tcv=T-alpha1*r1+R. Unbiased; variance reduction iff 2Cov(T,alpha1*r1)>Var(alpha1*r1), not universal. Coefficient optimal Cov/Var with independent pilot; used coefficient one. Construct negative example T identically 0 but root random nonconstant (child cancels root), subtracting root increases variance. Bound for Tcv may be looser than hard; do not apply hard Hoeffding range to CV without proof.
Strong direct baseline D(X)=[mtheta1(X)(1+sum_y mtheta2(y|X))-m01(X)(1+sum_y m02(y|X))]/q01(X) estimates A_theta-A0 exactly in expectation, not surrogate H. Subtract sampled first-step difference (mtheta1-m01)/q01 and add exact root sum to get direct CV. Same available root+child rows, direct objective avoids Jensen conservatism. Importance weights can be unstable under big changes. No guarantee SCM estimator dominates direct. Both can be evaluated by ordinary teacher-forced draft/target logits on sampled q path without actually executing acceptance decisions, so ordinary same-information flat FL or AR training with auxiliary q can reproduce. Actual SD may already have old logits, but old/new parameter snapshots and recomputation cost must be measured.

## T: fixed-candidate confidence and hierarchy
If at each audited prefix the range of formal and draft logit changes is <= L, |log p_new(x)-log p_old(x)|<=L and same for q; min-log is 1-Lipschitz in max norm. Thus |l_t|<=L, |Z|<=B=L K(K+1)/2; conditional soft/terminal means stay in [-B,B]. For fixed workload strata c with weights w_c, n_c independent samples per stratum, estimate Hhat=sum_c w_c mean(Z_c). Hoeffding radius sqrt(2 log(1/delta) sum_c w_c^2 B_c^2/n_c). Client sends sum,count (and known range metadata); edge sums weighted means and squared radius contributions, cloud sums; hierarchy is associative and flat equivalent. Require Hhat-radius>=0, plus every client's held-out CE change upper confidence bound <= epsilon_c. For paired NLL changes log p ratio bounded by L_c, radius L_c sqrt(2 log(1/delta_c)/n_c), use independent guard draws, union bound across clients and candidate trials with fixed prespecified budgets or fresh validation. These statements assume bounded logit drift on full declared audit support, not just sampled maximum, and independent samples; actual rollout temporal correlations or adaptive candidates need separate concentration. For fixed context strata and common B, equal samples yields radius B sqrt(2 log(1/delta)/N). Oracle radius-equals-true-H scale N=ceil(2B^2 log(1/delta)/H^2) is NOT a guaranteed power/sample-size requirement or achieved certificate; a larger margin and power analysis required. If CV estimate used, recalculate range (simple triangle up to 3B, or sharper known terms). Cost-optimal independent stratification n_c proportional w_c sigma_c/sqrt(cost_c) from Cauchy-Schwarz, classical Neyman allocation, not new. Need actual per-client distributions/variance estimates and pilot costs. No validated confidence coverage experiment.
