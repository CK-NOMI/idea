## 1. Architecture and fixed feature contract
Frozen common causal Transformer, shallow/deep row features X_r,Y_r, output head W (d×V, code stores transpose), region bridge M_r (d×d), common bridge M0. Target p=softmax(yW), endogenous q_r=softmax(x M_r W). Not separate full draft LM. Clients hold authorized nonIID calibration/training data; moments can leak, no DP/security claim. Region bridges personalized, cloud W shared. Unlike prior FCRH (global ridge U=MW), propose hierarchical regularization and eliminate U variables from a coupled objective. TJSA/FSRA already studied shared low-rank factorization; no novelty claim for that. R26/FSGPA already studied target protection and multiobjective training; distinguish explicit minimized ridge penalty. Fixed feature assumption is essential; no arbitrary full-model updates or real multi-step/asynchronous HFL equivalence.

## 2. T: closed-form hierarchy, eliminated prediction objective
Let weights omega_r>0 sum1; G_r=X_r^T X_r/n_r, H_r=X_r^T Y_r/n_r, T_r=Y_r^T Y_r/n_r. Define A_r=(G_r+lambda I)^(-1), lambda,gamma>0.
J(M)=sum_r omega_r[||X_r M_r-Y_r||_F^2/n_r +lambda||M_r-M0||_F^2]+gamma||M0||_F^2. Normal equations: M_r=A_r(H_r+lambda M0), (lambda+gamma)M0=lambda sum_r omega_r M_r. Hence [gamma I+lambda sum_r omega_r(I-lambda A_r)]M0=lambda sum_r omega_r A_r H_r. Positive definite since G_r PSD, eigenvalues I-lambda A_r>=0 and gamma>0. Unique solution; additive unnormalized moments and counts are sufficient, averaging local inverse solutions not generally correct. This is classical hierarchical ridge/Schur elimination, not a new foundational theorem.
For W arbitrary, define J_W(U0,U_r)=sum_r omega_r[||X_r U_r-Y_r W||_F^2/n_r+lambda||U_r-U0||_F^2]+gamma||U0||_F^2. Same linear normal equations per output column imply U_r*=M_r W, U0*=M0 W, unique. Substitution gives min_U J_W=tr(W^T S W) where S=sum_r omega_r[(Y_r-X_r M_r)^T(Y_r-X_r M_r)/n_r+lambda(M_r-M0)^T(M_r-M0)]+gamma M0^T M0 PSD; can calculate from G,H,T. In fixed calibration setting min objective is convex quadratic in W and grad=2SW, Hessian PSD. Train task CE+beta/V tr(W^T S W) (beta normalization in code), draft heads refresh analytically after each W change, exact same minimized logit-square objective as explicit refit. No guarantee for probability KL, TV or exact EAL objective; choosing W may sacrifice formal quality. Counterexample W=0 gives uniform p=q, acceptance1, but arbitrary nonuniform label task worsens. Shrinkage alone may improve acceptance; match task quality and compare L2 and KL. New target model at each update, not lossless replacement of original pretrained model; SD exact only relative to deployed current p. Standard actual q/p min ratio and positive residual, independent randomness, causal truncation and bonus/EOS imply sequence law; greedy uses p argmax. No real generation kernel, top-p or finite precision proof/experiment here.
HFL learns region-specific moments influencing q; minimized draft loss influences formal W, algebraic two-way coupling. However same-info delegated flat can solve same linear system and train same W; ordinary personalized regression plus joint logit distillation can implement equivalent mechanism. Exact optimizer elimination does not establish architectural necessity. Single fixed-snapshot aggregate gradient in experiment is equal to flat average; no claim HFL-specific training advantage.

## 3. Cost and failure conditions
G,H,T require up to 3d^2 scalars per region/client (symmetric matrices can be packed); d=4096 FP32 dense upper count 201.33 MB (decimal), S ~67.11 MB, not lower bound. One-time fitting may amortize over many head updates, but feature drift forces refit, d^3 solve, d^2V head materialization vs d^2 per draft token direct bridge; storing local U of dV may be substantial. Low-rank/fixed feature projections may reduce cost only after approximation quality measured. Strong comparator retains same p and local/global identity-centered bridge or KL head, uses same moment amortization if applicable, ordinary optimized self-SD; no real runtime, communication or memory profile. Acceptance gains do not imply throughput. No actual shared trunk learning.
