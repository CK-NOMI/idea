## Reverse audit: three barriers
(1) Same information, compute and permissions: flat can simulate a hierarchical protocol; do not require mathematically impossible universal separation. (2) R31 target-first reconstruction and R90 committed ancestor subset: fixed-path cache/tape/observed randomness alone cannot establish exclusive SD learning advantage. New question: can a sparse conservative *formal parameterization*, rather than a post hoc coupling, redistribute mass across groups and make exact verification, hard-label gradients, and convex hierarchical aggregation share the same edge coefficients? ## T1 Model and exact kernel
For fixed prefix h, q_i>0 from endogenous predictor, graph E directed before proposal randomness; partition D/R disjoint, arcs D→R. Let a_ij(h)>=0, sum_j a_ij<=rho<1, p_i=q_i(1−sum_j a_ij)+sum_k q_k a_ki. This is a sparse row-stochastic map p=qT, not old teacher. Sample X~q, accept with prob 1−sum_j a_Xj, else select j with prob a_Xj/sum_j a_Xj. Output marginal p by column sums. Because D/R disjoint, p_i<=q_i on D, >=q_i on R, so accept mass=Σmin(p,q), TV=Σ q_i a_ij. No full residual or destination probability is needed in repair. Kernel is maximal coupling but not canonical independent positive-residual coupling; causal autoregressive prefix truncation using independent local draws preserves sequence distribution, no bonus required. Exact greedy needs global argmax, raw temperature/top-p may invalidate graph construction, EOS/finite precision not tested.
If arbitrary E allows vertex inflow I_i and outflow O_i simultaneously, one-hop T remains exact but rejection exceeds TV by Σ_i min(I_i,O_i), since TV=1/2 Σ|I−O| and total in=out=F. E.g. q=(1/3,1/3,1/3), equal positive flow 1→2 and 2→3, one-hop rejects 2f, TV=f. This is a real restriction when mixing client-specific directions.
## T2 Formal gradients and aggregation
Fixed q and directly parameterized a: ∂(-log p_y)/∂a_ij=q_i(1{y=i}−1{y=j})/p_y. For hard labels only incident edges active (for shared nonlinear parameter network sparsity may be lost); for soft teacher all edges potentially active. CE convex in a on product row-simplex. With common fixed q, same graph, convex client weights w, weighted averaging of a preserves feasibility, exactly averages p and joint coupling matrices, and with common donor/receiver signs TV also averages. If q_c differs, mixture Q_i=Σw q_ci, define a*_ij=Σw q_ci a_cij / Q_i (zero if Q_i=0); exact aggregate joint law, conditional probability-weighted, not ordinary FedAvg. Numerators/denominators are additive, tree composable; q_c depends on context, so actual federated aggregation of model parameters does NOT in general realize it, and communication may cost O(V+E) per context. Common orientation yields maximal aggregate coupling; opposed flows may cancel, same-delegation flat can implement. No learning-speed proof.
## T3 Capacity / quality certificate
For desired teacher r, exact representation by fixed directed bipartite E iff all nonzero surplus q_i−r_i nodes are donors, all deficits receivers, each donor surplus <=rho q_i, and all surplus can be routed to deficits. In source→donor→receiver→sink flow, source capacities min(rho q_i,(q_i−r_i)_+), sink capacities (r_i−q_i)_+, arc capacities unbounded. Max-flow/min-cut is classical; the value M is the maximum mass of a partial correction using only true surplus/deficit, with TV(r,p_partial)=TV(r,q)−M. In bipartite graph where no node both sends and receives, wrong-sign or overshooting arcs can be removed to reach a correction of at least as good TV: delete flows with wrong-sign source/dest (cannot decrease target TV benefit); then trim each donor to its useful capacity and each receiver to its deficit (deleting from an overshooting endpoint can lose at most the same benefit at the other). Thus this is also min TV for fixed bipartite graph. For undirected teacher-oracle orientation only true surplus→deficit used; no claim global optimality over arbitrary cyclic maps. A fixed orientation can fail on two contexts with opposite signs. Shared orientation makes convex aggregation exact but constrains quality. This is a structural tension, not mere optimizer failure.
