## 1. Problem, information and computational object
Fixed shared output W with row w_i, deep h and endogenous shallow s from same Transformer; p=softmax(Wh), q=softmax(Ws), original formal target (not Born/spherical/new density). For CE on an observed fixed prefix and label y, gradient wrt h is E_{Y~p} w_Y - w_y. For trainable earlier adapter, multiply by exact Jacobian transpose at the fixed observed prefix; W frozen. Does not estimate gradient of reward depending on realized Y by replacing w_Y alone, nor claim dense W gradient savings. Clients have different private prefix distributions; authorized sufficient aggregate covariance and edge scores only. No DP/secure aggregation implementation or necessary physical constraint demonstrated. q and deep h already computed in proposed joint workload, not a separate LLM. Learn orthogonal certification subspace U from residual delta=h-s statistics, and a disjoint token pairing from a certified common-submeasure objective. During actual SD acceptance, mark a subset of accept events and analytically integrate their learning gradients without changing emitted token or p. Formal training is fixed-context supervised CE, not full online RL.
## 2. T: original-Softmax acceptance certificate
Let P=UU^T, a_i=w_i^T P delta, b_i=||(I-P)w_i|| ||(I-P)delta||. Cauchy-Schwarz gives a_i-b_i <= w_i^T delta <= a_i+b_i. With full actual q available, Z=E_q exp(w_i^T delta), Z_U=sum q_i exp(a_i+b_i) >= Z; hence p_i/q_i >= exp(a_i-b_i)/Z_U. Let c_i=min(1,exp(a_i-b_i)/Z_U). If X~q and independent uniform u<=c_X, exact standard SD acceptance is known without full deep output projection; otherwise evaluate dense p and reuse the SAME u to decide min(1,p_X/q_X). On rejection sample normalized (p-q)_+, never independently resample original p. This is exactly ordinary maximal coupling in real arithmetic because accepted mass min(p_i,q_i) and correction mass [p_i-q_i]_+; sequential actual-prefix conditional induction with first-reject truncation and legal bonus/EOS preserves target sequence distribution. Greedy: w_i^T s+a_i +/- b_i gives a logit interval; certify candidate if its lower bound strictly exceeds all other upper bounds, else exact dense argmax with identical tie-break. No greedy/multitoken/KV production test. Temperature positive can be incorporated by rescaling logits with real arithmetic; top-p, penalties, finite precision, underflow and rounding need exact fallback/bounds before production. Numerical test observed roundoff 3.33e-16, not a bit-exact certificate. Do not use raw q after truncation.
## 3. T: certified common mass and learning variance, matching
For disjoint pairs g={i,j}, choose a_g=min(c_i,c_j) so l_i=a_g q_i and l_j=a_g q_j are dominated by min(p,q). Mark event L_g iff X in g and u<=a_g; it implies standard acceptance, probability a_g(q_i+q_j); Y conditional on L_g is q restricted to g. Replace w_Y by m_g=(q_i w_i+q_j w_j)/(q_i+q_j) only on L_g, otherwise leave w_Y. Output Y is unchanged. E[G]=E_p w_Y-w_y for fixed observed label y. By law of total covariance, Cov(w_Y)-Cov(G)=sum_g Pr(L_g) Cov_q(w|g) PSD; trace decrease per pair is a_g q_i q_j/(q_i+q_j) ||w_i-w_j||^2. This is Rao-Blackwellization, not a new general variance theorem. If exact p available replace c by min(1,p/q) for oracle; largest legal shared a_g maximizes pair gain. Regional edge scores are sums of above gains over observed training contexts; add them before maximum-weight matching. For fixed U and disjoint pairs with prescribed bipartite sets, additive objective permits global exact assignment; sample-dependent conditioning remains valid if pair choice before current X/u. In prototype exact subset-DP 13x13 matching, not scalable. PCA residual second moment sum_e C_e is additive; pooled power iteration used in code, not actual federated communication. Same-info flat has identical covariance/assignment. PCA minimizes average squared residual, not certificate loss or actual wall time.
## 4. T: strongest direct-AR replacement and counterexamples
AR draws Y~p, records its pair g, replaces w_Y by E_p[w|g]. Its per-pair variance decrease is p_i p_j/(p_i+p_j)||w_i-w_j||^2. For each a_g q_i<=p_i, a_g q_j<=p_j; xy/(x+y) is increasing in both arguments. Therefore AR with same partition and full p weakly dominates the candidate in variance reduction, for any p,q. It may need full projection but fused exact sampling and direct lazy sampling with same bounds are necessary baselines. Also direct target mixture p=sum_g l_g + residual produces identical tag and Y joint law with no speculative multi-token execution; its cost must be compared, not assume HFL or SD indispensable. Rank d gets maximal common certificate but no projection savings, q=p still may give weak certificate if loose subspace/radius; greedy equality alone doesn't prove hidden gradient correctness. Counterexample to acceptance as gradient value: two pairs with W rows nearly identical in first pair and far apart in second; high accepted mass in first can have near-zero learning variance gain. Matching weights must include squared distance; for full parameter gradients use Jacobian-induced metric, not measured here.

## 6. P: realistic cost and scaling obstacles
Optimistic target-head dot proxy r/d+(1-early)(1-r/d), assumes incremental residual coordinates, excludes projection into U, norm/exp reductions, q, memory, full transformer, rollback, batch/kernel inefficiency. Rank4 ~38.21% reduction ONLY target-head dot work; rank8 ~38.11%. Even with zero overhead that head must be >=39.3% of baseline total time to reach 15% savings. No measured portion. Block parallel verification, residual correction and bonus may require full GEMM anyway; batch rows not independent costs. PCA covariance d4096 full symmetric FP32 33.56MB per region, plus U distribution, not free. Dense 128k vocab bipartite edge scores ~4.096e9 floats=16.384GB per region; unacceptable. Sparse candidate graph with bounded edges O(Vk), randomized covariance sketch, per-region U, or public codebook are H only; need matched flat & non-adaptive PCA/random/quantized baselines and actual kernel. W updating would require refresh W projection and norm; frozen W only. No claim formal quality preserved across different learned optimizer trajectories; exact target at each snapshot is preserved.
