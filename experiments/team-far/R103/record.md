## T: exact partial-vocabulary lower quality bound
Fixed legal prefix, snapshot, full-support reference p0, fixed draft q, candidate p defined by exact logits (temperature 1, no truncation). Let S proper nonempty subset, t=p0(S), u=q(S), s=p(S), r=p(.|S) computable from only candidate rows in S. KL chain identity KL(p0||p)=BerKL(t||s)+t KL(p0(.|S)||r)+(1-t) KL(p0(.|Sc)||p(.|Sc)). Hence L=t KL(p0(.|S)||r) lower bound; L is nondecreasing along nested S by chain rule restricted to larger subset; at full vocabulary exact. If L>eta, reject for this prefix without full normalization. For average-quality guard over fixed contexts/clients, use weighted sum of lower bounds, not reject based on one prefix alone unless per-prefix guard prescribed. Additive statistics can be summed on authorized region/cloud; no formal privacy guarantee or hierarchy advantage.
## T: exact relaxed acceptance upper
A(p,q)=sum_i min(p_i,q_i). Given known r and unrestricted candidate outside S, for any s, A<=F(s)=sum_{i in S} min(s r_i,q_i)+min(1-s,1-u). Tight given s because outside S choose conditional q(.|Sc). For s<=u the second term constant and first nondecreasing; for s>=u second term decreases at unit slope and first increases with slope <=1. Thus maximum at s=u, A<=1-u TV(r,q(.|S)). This is tight under known conditional r alone (choose s=u and outside q). If this upper falls below exact incumbent score, safe elimination. A partial subset is used only for offline evaluation, never truncate deployment sampling as if p unchanged.
