## T: simultaneous maximality feasibility for nonlaminar groups
For finite product Q,P on labelled vectors and any family F of subsets of coordinates, projection x_S,y_S. Let D_S=TV(Q_S,P_S). Every coupling has r_S=Pr(X_S!=Y_S)>=D_S. Equality iff off-diagonal projected mass only goes from Q_S>P_S to P_S>Q_S (diagonal min fixed). Therefore simultaneous equality iff there exists a transport with support graph E={(x,y): for every S, either x_S=y_S or Q_S(x_S)>P_S(x_S) and P_S(y_S)>Q_S(y_S)}. This holds for any finite marginals, not just products. Weighted Hall/maxflow criterion: for every subset A of source states Q(A)<=P(N_E(A)), equivalent to feasible unit flow. Deficit delta=max_A[Q(A)-P(N_E(A))] (empty set yields 0) equals 1-maxflow. For any coupling, mass outside E>=delta. For each S, let eps_S=r_S-D_S=sum_z(min(P_S,Q_S)-J_S(z,z)). Off-diagonal mass departing non-donor states <=eps_S and arriving non-recipient states <=eps_S. Union bound gives delta<=2 sum_S eps_S. Thus simultaneous exactness obstruction has quantitative certificate. No claim all crossing families impossible, or polynomial complexity in number of coordinates; graph exponential. Classical flow/Hall applied to SD projection constraints, not new general mathematics.
Counterexample three binary lanes each q(1)=1/4,p(1)=3/4. All two-lane mixed states (01,10) have identical marginal masses. From x=001, flipping either zero violates maximality on its pair with lane3; leaf maximality forbids flipping 1 to 0. So x has no nonidentity allowed destination despite Q(x)=9/64>P(x)=3/64. Simultaneous impossible. All pair TV=1/2, leaf TV=1/2, root TV=44/64=.6875. For sum of pair event costs C=0,2,3,3 when Hamming distance d=0,1,2,3. Exact optimum pair cost 114/64=1.78125>1.5. Proof dual feasible f(x)=3,2,0,0 by Hamming weight (|f(x)-f(y)|<=C), E_Q f-E_P f=114/64. Achieve by retaining diagonal min, sending surplus 26/64 from 000 to111 and each surplus 6/64 of weight1 states to distinct weight2 supersets (cycle e.g.001->011,010->110,100->101). This also attains root and leaf lower bounds. Unavoidable pair cost excess 18/64=.28125 (18.75% of unattainable lower bound), mean pair repair .59375. R70 star pair cost1.95703125. Hall deficit .28125 via actual flow audit. Not a claim about standard tree HFL.
## T: topology selection and strong oracle
Cost C(x,y)=sum_S b_S 1{x_S!=y_S}, nonnegative weights; a pseudometric. By triangle inequality, any flow path through intermediate states can be compressed, retaining min diagonal without increasing cost (finite flow path/cycle decomposition). Thus there exists optimum with maximal root coupling. Residual transport min-cost flow on surplus Q-P and deficit P-Q gives exact small-state oracle; does not automatically preserve leaf optimality, although our experiments include unit leaf cost and observed leaf lower bound. R72 root+leaf+chosen compatible laminar groups maximal. For any pair ij not chosen, r_ij<=u_ij=min(D_i+D_j,D_root). For chosen pairs r_ij=D_ij. To minimize certified upper bound choose disjoint pairs via maximum weight matching with edge score b_ij(u_ij-D_ij), root and singletons fixed. For arbitrary hyperedges maximum-weight compatible laminar set harder; no polynomial guarantee claimed. Selected tree can be sampled via R72 implicit kernel without full joint enumeration, but accurate D_ij and D_root are nontrivial to estimate; D_root exact product TV computational barrier, common upper bound 1 or sum leaf TV possible but weaker. Selection must be causal before seeing candidate RNG. This is an upper-bound optimizer, not global OT optimum or proof best tree. Physical feasibility, privacy, grouping control messages and true HFL learning not established. If topology predetermined, only permitted matchings; a flat scheduler with same information and delegated resources can implement.
