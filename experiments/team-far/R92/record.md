## T1 formal distribution and quality frontier
Fixed prefix and positive shared endogenous q, regional correction mask S fixed before current candidate randomization. Define p_i=q_i outside S and p_i=q(S) softmax_S(log q_i+delta_i) inside S. This is a new formal target, not exact to original teacher r. For full-support r,q minimize KL(r||p)+lambda TV(p,q) over simplex. TV=sum_i(p_i-q_i)_+. KKT gives p_i=r_i/(nu+lambda) if r_i>(nu+lambda)q_i, r_i/nu if r_i<nu q_i, otherwise q_i. Unique minimizer by strict convexity. nu in [0,1] by endpoint mass and monotonicity; zero endpoint uses limit. lambda=0 returns r. Classical reverse projection and clipped likelihood ratios; not new basic optimization. Objective <= min(KL(r||q),lambda TV(r,q)).
## T2 cardinality support bound
For fixed S, KL minimizer with p outside S=q is p_i=q(S)r_i/r(S) within S, by Lagrange multiplier. Let d(a,b)=a log(a/b)-a+b. Improvement G(S)=sum_S d(r_i,q_i)-d(r(S),q(S)); hence KL >= max(0, KL(r||q)-sum of k largest d_i) for |S|<=k. Common support across contexts uses k largest weighted average d_i, additive tree reduction. G monotone by log-sum inequality, not generally submodular: q=(1/3,1/3,1/3), r=(1/6,1/6,2/3), adding second coordinate to {first} gains zero; adding it to {first,third} gains positive. Greedy not globally optimal; exact pair initialization, exhaustive k4, nested greedy k8+. The fixed q(S) group mass imposes an irreducible Bernoulli KL term. Full teacher oracle unavailable free at inference.
## T3 exact verification and regional learning
Standard SD candidate X~q: outside S accept without regional correction, inside compute p_S and accept min(1,p_X/q_X), otherwise sample positive residual supported inside S. Conditional marginal p and standard causal truncation/bonus imply exact new formal sequence in ideal arithmetic. Bonus requires legal p draw. Greedy needs exact full argmax; production top-p/EOS/FP not tested. Direct AR can sample X~q, return outside S, resample p(.|S) inside; same marginal and correction activation q(S), so no SD-exclusive single-step work savings. For hard label outside S, CE correction gradient exactly zero when q independent of delta parameters. Inside S correction logits gradient softmax_S-e_y; soft teacher gradient r(S)softmax_S-r_i. Shared q gradients need not be sparse, mask selection and refresh costs are not free, physical hierarchy/flat same delegation not distinguished.
