## T1 — exact finite-bit protocol
Reuse R79 two independent pre-existing drafts, A first commits without B information, both leaf-maximal, output product target. s(y)=(p_A(y)-q_A(y))_+/p_A(y), t(x)=(q_B(x)-p_B(x))_+/q_B(x) on positive denominators, else 0. Given A's actual (Y=y,R), sample U uniform on [0,s(y)] if R=1, [s(y),1] if R=0 (skip probability-zero branches). Then U uniform conditional on Y, hence independent Y but correlated with R and original draft. Receiver X independent of A,U. Precommit partition I_k=[l_k,r_k), lengths w_k>0, send k (ceil(log2 m) payload bits). For receiver x, offdiagonal mass in stratum k t_k(x)=q_B(x)*length(I_k intersect [0,t(x)]), diagonal w_k q_B(x)-t_k(x), offdiag t_k(x)*(p_B(z)-q_B(z))_+/d_B (zero if d_B=0). Divide by w_k q_B(x) to sample conditional kernel. Sum k recovers ordinary maximal B kernel; since k independent Y_A and B's input independent A, output product exactly. Sender and receiver local repair marginal fixed. f(u)=sum_y p_A(y)1{s(y)>u}, g(u)=sum_x q_B(x)1{t(x)>u}. Let A_k=int_I f, B_k=int_I g. Joint repair M_partition=sum_k A_k B_k/w_k; union C=d_A+d_B-M. Continuous U yields M*=int f g=sum_yx p_A q_B min(s,t), equals R79 exact directional one-way optimum. Finite partition containing all breakpoints of f OR g suffices for equality, not both; at most vocab size+1 intervals (including endpoints; actual counts in results). Such adaptive exact partition requires receiver to know boundaries in advance, may cost O(V) setup, no free fixed-bit claim. This is a one-way conditional protocol, not arbitrary multi-message communication optimum.
## T2 — refinement, bound and offline calibration
Within I covariance(f,g)>=0 by identity cov=(1/2)E[(f(U)-f(U'))(g(U)-g(U'))], since both nonincreasing. Refining an interval likewise raises M by covariance of conditional mean curves. Gap M*-M=sum_k w_k cov_I(f,g) <= max(w_k)/4 * sum_k osc_I(f)*osc_I(g) <= max(w_k)/4 (Cauchy-Schwarz variance bound or Gruss, disjoint intervals and monotonic total variation <=1; ignore endpoints measure zero). Uniform m gives gap <=1/(4m), 0-bit m=1 independent. Optimal interval partition on fixed grid: maximize sum of interval scores S_ij=mean over calibration pairs[(A(j)-A(i))(B(j)-B(i))/(u_j-u_i)], using dp[k,j]=max_{i<j} dp[k-1,i]+S_ij. O(mK^2) after O(nK^2) score construction and local O(nKV) curve computation; optimal only among grid partitions, not globally optimal all possible messages. Same partition known both sides, no per-pair oracle.
