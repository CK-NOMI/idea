## 1. Mathematical object
Freeze W and Transformer hidden states h,s (full and own shallow layer, no independent drafter). Shared additive hidden shift theta, p=softmax(W(h+theta)), q=softmax(W(s+theta)), score vectors W_i-mu_p and W_i-mu_q, mu_p=sum p_i W_i etc. CE L=-log p_y on fixed labeled data. D=TV(p,q)=sum_{i:p_i>q_i}(p_i-q_i), overlap A=1-D (single-step standard maximal-coupling acceptance for the fixed context). No claim fixed-data overlap equals deployed multi-token throughput. For no ties, t=grad_theta D=sum_{i in P+}(p_i(W_i-mu_p)-q_i(W_i-mu_q)). At ties positive-part zero choice is a valid local Clarke subgradient, not differentiability or guaranteed directional descent. Define Q+=sum_{i in P+}q_i. In standard SD, probability of rejecting and emitting correction Y=i is (p_i-q_i)_+. Then t=E[1_reject (W_Y-mu_p)]+Q+(mu_q-mu_p). Proof split p_i score_p - q_i score_q=(p_i-q_i)score_p+q_i(mu_q-mu_p). Correction is necessary and itself needs p,q. Conditional trace variance of sampled estimator v=sum_{P+}(p_i-q_i)||W_i-mu_p||² - ||sum_{P+}(p_i-q_i)(W_i-mu_p)||². Direct analytic t is conditional mean, hence covariance no greater, exactly eliminates trace sampling variance given p,q,W. If p,q already available in standard verification, direct sum is strong deterministic replacement, also available without SD random events by evaluating both heads. No claim faster wall time. This is restricted shared shift; not full transformer parameter gradient. 
## 2. Formal learning selection and finite-step protection
At fixed theta and finite calibration contexts, average CE gradient g and TV subgradient t, with client sample-weighted aggregation (equal weights in actual data). Select active coordinates S, |S|=r, optimize sum_{j in S} g_j² subject to sum_{j in S} g_j t_j>=0. Update direction -P_S g gives CE directional derivative -||P_S g||² and, at differentiable points, TV derivative -sum g_j t_j. If no feasible support, exact method minimizes violation; no universal feasibility guarantee. Coordinate subsets exhaustively enumerated only d16; not scalable. Across rounds masks can change and theta accumulates inactive coordinates, so r is active-update budget, NOT final parameter rank/sparsity, NOT LoRA/actual growing network. Region averages additive; same-information flat computes identical selection and can emulate regional delegated computations. Client SGD after choice is not exact global direction and finite steps can violate TV. 
Second version: on CE top8 support, projected direction d=-g_S if g_S·t_S>=0, otherwise d=-g_S+(g_S·t_S/||t_S||²)t_S for nonzero t_S. For differentiable D this is projection of -g_S onto halfspace t_S·d<=0; g·d<=0, may be zero; at ties no guarantee. This is standard gradient surgery, not a new theorem. Search candidate directions d-lambda*t_S*||g_S||/||t_S|| with lambda in {0,.05,.2,.5,1} and eta in {.6,.3,.15}. Evaluate exact empirical CE/TV before committing, choose lowest CE feasible; if none, no update. Global guard requires empirical average D not increase and L strictly decrease; regional guard requires each region's calibration D nonincrease and mean L decrease. Guarantees only evaluated finite calibration objective, no population guarantee. Fixed finite data, tolerance1e-12. Regional constraints stronger than pooled and may preclude improvement; e.g. t1=(1,0),t2=(-1,0),g=(-1,0) forces d1=0 so no strict CE descent. Direct gradient/projection/line search can operate without SD; same-info flat with per-region stats identical. No G2.
