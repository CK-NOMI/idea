## 1. Setting and novelty boundary
Real Fed-GRPO R31 fixed source a27d5c9366b91583d5a1b4a09d0dda00afe0e5df uses per-client local GRPO and reward-signal weighted aggregation, not necessarily cross-client groups. Here deliberately define a new across-client group-normalized, single on-policy score update with N independently sampled trajectories Y_i from heterogeneous distributions P_i(theta), common comparable binary reward R_i(Y_i) and full score S_i=grad log P_i(Y_i), fixed snapshot. Distinct prompts/tasks permissible only if their binary success semantics and weights intentionally define this new objective. Not conventional per-prompt GRPO or arbitrary multi-epoch clipped policy loss, not claim this deployment already exists. No private data transfer required by scalar algebra but scalar statistics disclose information; no privacy guarantee. Define K=sum R_i; use population std sqrt((K/N)(1-K/N)), zero advantage for all-equal outcomes; G=(1/N)sum A_i S_i. Exact SD via actual internal q_i at causal prefixes, maximum coupling and positive residual; no change to formal law. Physical needs for cross-client group, shared reward semantics and cost remain unverified. R24 anchored subsampling and FCTG exact realized group clipping are close but different: this work integrates over heterogeneous independent population reward probabilities. R30 count convolution adjoints reused, not new general probability primitive.

## 2. T: heterogeneous population potential
Let pi_i=E R_i, grad pi_i=E[R_i S_i], E S_i=0 for fixed differentiable full-support distribution and bounded parameter-independent reward. Given K_-i=k, conditional expected contribution of i is d_N(k) grad pi_i, where d_N(k)=sqrt((N-1-k)/(k+1))+sqrt(k/(N-k)), k=0,...,N-1 (zero terms at ends naturally implement all-equal rule). This follows by subtracting advantage A_i(1,k+1) and A_i(0,k). Hence
E G=(1/N)sum c_i(pi_-i) grad pi_i, c_i=E_{K_-i}d_N(K_-i).
Define Phi(0)=0, Phi(k+1)-Phi(k)=d_N(k)/N. Then E G=grad_theta E[Phi(K)] by differentiating independent Bernoulli law with respect to pi_i and chain rule. This proves a scalar potential for heterogeneous unweighted equal-group on-policy binary normalized update, not general clipped GRPO, length normalization, nonlinear aggregation, dependent rewards or arbitrary client-specific weights. Homogeneous iid limit is covered by prior population GRPO literature, so novelty limited to explicit heterogeneous formulation + estimator/cost audit. Local independent groups generally have different potential; example N=4, p=(.99,.99,.01,.01) vs N=2 local groups d_2=1, global d_4 values sqrt(3), sqrt(1/3)+1, same, sqrt(3); local and global coefficients differ, cannot declare equivalent objectives.
All c_i obtainable as derivative of F=E Phi(K) through product tree polynomial prod[(1-pi_i)+pi_i z]; adjoint convolution R30 gives O(N²) naive balanced arithmetic/O(N log N) coeff storage, unlike code's simple per-leaf leave-one-out O(N³). This is standard reverse differentiation of multilinear expectation. No need to share prompts/score vectors for coefficients if permitted to exchange scalar pi or polynomial coefficients and adjoints; actual pi estimation/transport and downstream aggregate gradient communication not free. Same-info flat can perform same operations. Formula applies to deterministic probabilities; for unbiased random estimators below, signed pseudo-coefficients need not be probabilities; no clipping without bias, possible numerical cancellation.

## 3. T: coupled reward control variates
Let X_i be complete q_i draft trajectory, Y_i formal trajectory coupled by standard max-coupling on shared prefix and independent legal q continuation after first mismatch (not free, q draft after reject is not the formal trajectory). U_i=R_i(X_i), mu_i=E U_i. For pre-fixed beta_i define Z_i=R_i(Y_i)-beta_i(U_i-mu_i). Then E Z_i=pi_i. If client pairs independent, substitute Z into each multilinear c_i; c_i(Z_-i) is unbiased, independent of own (Y_i,X_i); use H=(1/N)sum c_i(Z_-i)(R_i(Y_i)-b_i)S_i with fixed/detached baseline b_i, then E H=E G. Can use plain Z=R without SD (strong sampled-coeff baseline); known exact pi gives oracle deterministic c. Need actual q reward mean and independent clients; do not reuse correlated q mean or fit beta using same current sample without proof. Since each coefficient is a multi-affine polynomial in independent variables, centered ANOVA decomposition yields Var(c_i(Z)) = sum over nonempty subsets of squared expansion coefficients at pi times product of coordinate variances; if every Z variance decreases vs Bernoulli R, each c_i variance nonincreases and own contribution variance nonincreases by independence. Does not prove total aggregated variance nonincrease: coefficients and other clients' scores share randomness, covariances matter. beta*=Cov(R,U)/Var(U) minimizes scalar variance; beta=1 may be worse, example pi=.01,mu=.5, maximal binary coupling gives Var(R-U)=.2499 vs Var(R)=.0099. For single exact-token reward joint success min(pi,mu), for two-token exact pair reward under prefix coupling joint success product of local min probabilities at correct prefix. No general reward-class maximality for arbitrary rewards. AR+R31 reverse reconstruction with q suffix can match full law and is strong comparator; no unique SD statistical gain.
When mu unknown estimate by independent M q-only trajectories Tbar, E=mu, fixed beta independent of all draws. Z=R-beta(U-Tbar) is unbiased. Optimal beta_M=Cov(R,U)/(Var(U)(1+1/M)), scalar variance Var(R)-Cov²/(Var(U)(1+1/M)). Additional q-only generation and reward evaluation costs nonzero. Test uses oracle beta and exact mu as own baseline even for M, so optimistic rather than deployable. Baseline can use fixed constant or independent estimate without affecting expectation, must re-evaluate variance. For full task reward may require q rollout beyond each speculative block; this work uses two-token horizon only. No claim exact complete production sequence/greedy/top-p/FP. Usual exact SD with postprocessed p,q, causal prefix, correct residual, EOS and bonus is the target contract; actual short experiment omits bonus and uses independent continuation at divergence.
