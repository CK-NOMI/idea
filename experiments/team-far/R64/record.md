## T/P: joint service frontier, not a new kernel
Define fixed concurrency b, draft length k=0..8, independent constant conditional survival alpha (artificial), expected formal committed tokens g_k=b sum_{j=0}^k alpha^j, including bonus when legal, k=0 ordinary AR. Verify b(k+1) target rows. Frozen W size mn elements BF16, one ideal read 2mn bytes, forward/backward per row 2mn FLOPs. Let rho=P/BW in effective rows; at nominal A100 peak rho=312000/1555=200.643. Use effective rho64/128 plus nominal, not measured. Training ready reverse rows m=0,16,...512. Optimistic time normalized to one weight scan:
t(k,m)=max(1,[b(k+1)+m]/rho)+d k+0.05*1{m>0}. Separate training-only t(-1,m)=max(1,m/rho), no draft. d synthetic cost per draft step, constant across b, overhead assumption not measurements. Counts only one representative frozen linear layer; actual attention/KV, activation IO, tile reduction, LoRA gradients, launch, synchronization, token dependence, forward training, rewards, memory residency, optimizer/communication and stochastic queueing omitted. Ideal exact same-weight fusion may not attain peak. Cannot use LayerLoom authors' throughput as measured inputs. Under saturated compute and fixed t ignoring overhead/draft increment, additional speculative token per sequence consumes b more forward rows and displaces b reverse rows; marginal expected formal token benefit b alpha^(k+1). With utility lambda per ready backward row, increasing k in compute-bound fixed-budget case only increases weighted output if alpha^(k+1)>lambda, assuming m>=b available and fractional model; positive draft cost makes threshold stricter. Memory-bound slack can permit both until exhaustion. Ordinary AR also has access to this sharing.
For fixed required expected formal generation G>0 and reverse rows M>=0, choose nonnegative continuous action counts n_a to minimize sum n_a t_a, subject to sum n_a g_a=G and sum n_a m_a=M. Two equality constraints => basic optimum supported by at most two actions (if feasible; all times positive, objective bounded, finite grid). Set G=1 and R=M/G; for g>0 transform x=m/g,y=t/g, feasible convex mixtures on lower convex hull and training-only ray of cheapest slope t/m. Enumerate one generating action + training-only or two generating actions bracketing R. Exact LP for this restricted deterministic fluid model, not integer finite trajectory scheduling, real SGD convergence, or stochastic deadlines. HFL edge chooses locally and cloud waits/aggregates under fixed snapshots; no training objective changes, no new update, equal-information flat/delegated system reproduces. Strong independent fixed k with same fusion, tuned to R, and fully flexible load-aware co-serving are mandatory; full flexible scheduler can exactly reproduce candidate action set.
