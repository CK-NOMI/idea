## T1 — zero communication under pre-existing independent drafts
Fix causal frontier and positive finite p_i,q_i. X_i~q_i independently; common random U independent of X, private independent randomization conditional on U; local Y_i uses only X_i,U and private random. Require Y~product p and each leaf maximal. Donor D_i={x:q_i(x)>p_i(x)}. Maximality forces every offdiagonal transition from donor to recipient almost surely. R_i=1{X_i!=Y_i} therefore equals 1{X_i in D_i}-1{Y_i in D_i}. Conditional on U, local pairs independent and E[R_i|U]=q_i(D_i)-Pr(Y_i in D_i|U). For every subset S, expand E product of these differences. Product output law makes each mixed moment E product Pr(Y_i in D_i|U)=product p_i(D_i). Hence E product R_i=product TV_i and all repair indicators jointly independent. No communication cannot improve the group repair probability 1-product(1-TV_i) in this class. Not a universal communication lower bound: draft generated using common U, output dependence, nonmaximal leaves or nonlocal state can invalidate assumptions.
## T2 — exact one-way optimum
Two independent pre-existing drafts, A commits Y_A without any B information, B may learn A's state; final product p_A*p_B and both leaf couplings maximal. s(y)=Pr(R_A=1|Y_A=y)=(p_A(y)-q_A(y))_+/p_A(y); skip zero mass. X_B independent of (Y_A,R_A), since A cannot see X_B and common randomness independent of drafts. Conditional on each Y_A=y, X_B has q_B and Y_B has p_B; B's offdiagonal outgoing donor mass per x is d(x)=(q_B(x)-p_B(x))_+. To maximize joint repair assign t1(x)=min(s(y)q_B(x),d(x)) to R_A=1 and t0=d-t1. Both nonnegative and at most their stratum q_B capacity. In stratum weight w=s or 1-s, joint B mass diagonal w*q_B(x)-t(x), offdiag t(x)*(p_B(z)-q_B(z))_+/TV_B (zero if TV_B=0). This matches rows, and summed over strata columns p_B for each y; leaves maximal. Joint repair M=sum_y,x min((p_A(y)-q_A(y))_+ q_B(x),p_A(y)d(x)); union C=TV_A+TV_B-M. Capacity bounds prove optimal within stated one-way class (including shared U independent of drafts). Need convey R_A and s(Y_A) or equivalent public-table index; no claim on fixed bits for full optimum. For q=(.75,.25),p=(.25,.75) both leaves, independent .75, one-way .625, full product TV .5. No one-way protocol satisfying these constraints reaches full max. General one-way optimum is directional.
## T3 — single flag independent of sender formal token
Choose 0<tau<1 before draft draw (e.g. public fixed tau). Sender samples Z with Pr(Z=1|Y_A=y)=tau and joint conditional Pr(Z=1,R_A=1|Y_A=y)=min(tau,s(y)); feasible 2x2 table. Only send Z, not Y_A or s. Let A(tau)=sum_y min(tau*p_A(y),(p_A(y)-q_A(y))_+), B(tau)=sum_x min(tau*q_B(x),d(x)). B uses t1(x)=min(tau*q_B(x),d(x)), t0=d-t1, and above conditional kernel with weights tau,1-tau. Z independent Y_A, X_B independent sender/flag; mixture of receiver laws is p_B. Thus Y_A,Y_B independent with exact target and both local maximal. Joint repair A*B/tau+(TV_A-A)*(TV_B-B)/(1-tau); improvement vs independent = (A-tau*TV_A)*(B-tau*TV_B)/(tau*(1-tau))>=0 by min(tau,s)>=tau*s. At fixed tau this is optimal among protocols using only a binary flag and independent receiver private RNG with Z independent Y_A: Frechet bounds on joint flag/repair, choose aligned upper ends (or relabel flags/tau for lower ends). More generally if receiver conditional output laws differ, exact output independence forces Z independent Y_A; if identical p_B, maximality fixes repair probability and no gain, under the same receiver information model. Do not claim optimum with correlated pre-draft public randomness or arbitrary interactive messages. Grid search tau .01:.01:.99 is oracle selection per distribution, not deployed; fixed tau .5 available. Sender computes s from local p/q, receiver computes donor probability from local p/q, no p/q transfer online after precommitting tau/order. One payload bit is not one physical bit including RPC framing, scheduling, clock/version metadata; serialization is a new dependency.
Extension: apply to two independent subtree vectors, each with existing root-maximal coupling J (e.g. R72). Sender retains original J; receiver splits each donor state's offdiagonal mass into strata as above, preserving conditional destination J(x,.)/offdiag and overall entire subtree J. Hence exact child group laws and all existing R72 within-subtree maximalities survive, with one intersubtree flag. Parent root generally not maximal, so do NOT recursively claim R72 optimality or tractable s at subsequent nonmaximal parent.
