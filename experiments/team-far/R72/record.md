## T: laminar simultaneous maximum
Fixed causal frontier, positive finite categorical p_i,q_i (different lanes permitted), independent product P,Q, fixed rooted partition tree of leaves. At every node v, P_v,Q_v products of its children's laws. Define A_v=1-TV(P_v,Q_v). Claim a joint coupling J_root of Q,P projects at every node v to a maximal coupling J_v; indeed the entire (X_v,Y_v) pair law of each child is preserved. All internal nodes have nonidentity moves only from Q_v>P_v to P_v>Q_v. Base leaf ordinary maximal coupling. Induct: at v form product of child couplings J0, whose diagonal D_v(z)=prod_c min(P_c(z_c),Q_c(z_c)); offdiagonal graph acyclic, rank number of child coordinates in positive P_c-Q_c set. Use R70 implicit flow compression, retaining diag min(P_v,Q_v), initially reject only at Q_v>P_v, walk nonidentity edges from J0 conditional on current state, absorb on arrival with (P_v-Q_v)_+/(P_v-D_v). Edge utilization equals J0 offdiagonal: residual start (Q_v-P_v)+ plus incoming continuation min(I,O) equals O, by topological induction. Each changed child moves once (donor to recipient, so cannot change again). For each offdiag child pair (a,b), projected terminal pair mass equals sum of edge utilization with c transition a->b, equals J_c(a,b) by product structure. Diagonal then determined by row marginal Q_c. Thus all child pairs preserved, inductively all subtree maximalities. No claim for overlapping non-laminar groups or nonproduct formal laws.
Corollary: for all nonnegative b_v, coupling minimizes C_T=sum_v b_v 1{X_v!=Y_v}, optimal expectation=sum_v b_v TV(P_v,Q_v), among all couplings of fixed products. If leaf weights represent per-token costs, same theorem. This is additive event cost, not arbitrary critical path, convex congestion, makespan or real time. It also preserves individual token pair laws of ordinary SD (not merely expected count). Formal output product exactly in real arithmetic. Greedy use separate deterministic path; no production FP/top-p/EOS/async block implemented. ## T: implementation
At node v compute log likelihood ratio r_v=sum descendant r_i, a_c=min(1,exp(r_c)). To choose nonidentity product-child transition, pick first changed child j proportional to (prod_{k<j}a_k)(1-a_j), then later children independently reject with prob 1-a_c; recursively call child conditional nonidentity kernel only on rejected children. At internal arrival use D_v/P_v=prod min(1,Q_c/P_c), absorb formula above. Each node can be forced at most once per sample; at most degree(v) hops within v. Under whole root draw, projected child pair law preserved; because child invoked only on nonidentity and distribution of its starting state equals (Q_c-P_c)+, its internal flow has same unconditional use as standalone. Hence E L_v=1-prod_child A_c for each internal node (no need to calculate A_c in sampler). Total expected internal hops=sum_v (1-prod_c A_c) <=#internal nodes, not <=1 for entire tree. Caching ratios/updating affected child summaries feasible, not implemented; prototype recomputes ratios. No polynomial exact TV evaluation claim. Distributed scalar summaries may require synchronized per-hop messages, root WAN and privacy; no network data or runtime benchmarks.
