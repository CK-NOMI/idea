## Formal problem and method
Frozen backbone and output W; trainable shared additive policy bias h_theta=h+U theta, s_theta=s+U theta. U fixed orthonormal basis from training-only residual second moment, rank r. Target p=softmax(W h_theta), endogenous q=softmax(W s_theta), original softmax. A_i=U^T w_i, true policy score g_i=A_i-mu_p, mu_p=sum p_j A_j. Restricted parameter dimension r; no full LLM gradient or change to original formal objective at a given snapshot. R20 certificate: delta=h-s; z_i=A_i^T U^T delta; b_i=||(I-UU^T)w_i|| ||(I-UU^T)delta||; Z_U=sum q_i exp(z_i+b_i); c_i=min(1,exp(z_i-b_i)/Z_U)<=min(1,p_i/q_i). Projected proxy r0=softmax(W s_theta+z), m=sum r0_j A_j, g0_i=A_i-m. All scores manual, not differentiating sampling probabilities or certificate.
Choose tau in (0,1], predetermined on legal past/context, define l_i=(1-tau) q_i c_i. Sample X~q, u~Unif independent. If u<=(1-tau)c_X, mark M=1, commit X, skip full target projection, use g0_X. Else compute full p, standard SD using SAME u, if rejected sample residual (p-q)_+/TV; M=0. The mark doesn't change standard SD acceptance, so joint P(Y=i,M=1)=l_i, marginal Y~p. Then pi_i=P(M=0|Y=i)=1-l_i/p_i >= tau. On M=0 p_i is known and exact score computed; use ghat=g0_i+(g_i-g0_i)/pi_i. On M=1 use g0_i without computing pi or p. Conditional mean E[ghat|Y=i,h]=g_i, conditional covariance = (1/pi_i-1)(g_i-g0_i)(g_i-g0_i)^T. Proof by two-point expectation. For fixed proxy, deterministic fallback score and exact conditional mean this is the unique fallback value (and randomizing fallback cannot improve conditional squared error). Without correction, conditional bias=(l_i/p_i)(g0_i-g_i), can multiply action-dependent reward and distort policy gradient. tau=0 can create pi_i=0; no arbitrary missing exact score recovery on such event without extra information. Even high acceptance not a gradient difference bound (A/R09). tau>0 bound additional conditional trace <=(1/tau-1)||mu_p-m||². If reward weights R(Y), add E[p_i R_i² (1/pi_i-1)||mu_p-m||²]. At a fixed full fraction F and constant per-action reward magnitude, Jensen gives uniform pi best; for nonconstant reward w_i oracle clip sqrt(w_i/lambda) subject to 1-q_i c_i/p_i <=pi_i<=1 and sum p_i pi_i=F. Requires p and reward oracle, NOT candidate or free.
## Sequence correctness
Fixed theta snapshot, causal context and true conditional q, independent future proposal/acceptance RNG, first-reject truncation, correct p bonus. Reveal proposal candidates sequentially in mathematical filtration, not conditioning on unrevealed draft suffix; acceptance and mark use no future candidate. Conditional output kernel is p at each actual prefix including after a rejection and bonus. Since future actual emitted tokens have target kernel independent of past mark given current committed prefix (by induction over future positions, with internal execution state marginalized), conditional expectation of each corrected score given emitted trajectory and revealed past before current candidate/bonus equals its exact score. Bonus uses full exact score. Thus E[R(Y_1:T) sum ghat_t]=E[R(Y_1:T)sum g_t] for bounded reward of emitted sequence (or independent exogenous reward conditional on it), fixed pre-step state baseline, same snapshot. This is first on-policy REINFORCE expectation, NOT per-trajectory exact gradient, NOT multi-epoch PPO, GRPO clipping, likelihood ratio, entropy, KL, FedAdam nonlinear moments or reward dependent on internal latency/mark. Do not condition on full internal block layout (post-selection); covariance across time not claimed additive. Greedy interval certificate qlog_i+z_i +/- b_i; if strict lower of chosen candidate exceeds all other uppers then exact argmax, otherwise dense fallback same tie break. Exact in real arithmetic; numerical audit not finite-precision proof, no top-p or EOS. Temperature positive may rescale but not tested. 
## HFL mechanism and limits
Collect region residual covariance (additive) for shared PCA and region score-cost calibration (tau grid). Logical two-level shared-bias SGD. Same-info flat can compute same covariance and policy; local tau choice is not intrinsically HFL, all four regions selected tau=.1 in all six configurations so personalization not demonstrated. Same-info AR may first sample from certified common submeasure and residual to reproduce joint Y,M, use same conditional gradient; cannot claim single-token uniqueness. Standard AR full p+exact score has zero added conditional noise; ordinary SD + batched exact score and fused target head/FlashSampling strong baselines. New mechanism combines actual early certification with output-conditional policy score, but A/R09, HT and bounded sampling already related. No G2 proof.
