## T1 — fixed-order relay contract and construction
Given independent pre-existing drafts X_i~q_i and fixed positive finite target p_i at a causal frontier, local nodes act sequentially in a fixed order. State Z_i has precommitted positive masses w_k, independent of entire committed formal vector Y_1:i. Node i has only Z_(i-1), local X_i,p_i,q_i, independent private RNG (no additional shared RNG/state). Let H_i=1 if any prior/local repair; v_k=Pr(H_(i-1)=1,Z_(i-1)=k), sorted v_k/w_k decreasing. Initially v=0, Z_0 independent with masses w. For local x, split donor d_x=(q_x-p_x)_+ into t_k(x) greedily in rank order, cap w_k q_x, until exhausted. Joint masses conditional index: diagonal w_k q_x-t_k(x); offdiag t_k(x)(p_y-q_y)_+/TV(p,q). Divide by w_k q_x to sample given actual flag and draft (skip zero denominators). Summing k gives ordinary maximal local coupling, so Y_i~p_i. Incoming Z independent of past Y; current X/private RNG independent of past and flag. Thus Y_i independent past. To refresh flag, condition on Y_i=y, sort local events (R_i=1 first, then R_i=0 by old rank k), allocate successive mass to new flag bins of sizes w_j p_y. Use corresponding conditional randomized split given observed event. R=1 implies H_i=1; otherwise Pr(H_i=1|k,local event)=v_k/w_k. Rank sorting needs only ordering, not actual posterior v_k/w_k or remote p/q; reencoding maintains decreasing repair posterior by construction. Since new flag has distribution w given each y and entire past Y is independent of all local events, new flag is independent of full formal vector. Thus product outputs exact for fixed frontier. This repairs R80's nonmaximal-parent recursion problem by carrying a rank state rather than treating each partial subtree as maximal. Full prior draft-output pair law not independent, only target product and local coupling fixed. Fresh conditional frontier and fixed distributions/weights given past, no hidden-state leakage or lookahead, could yield multi-step conditional exactness by induction; not implemented. Greedy/top-p/EOS/production FP not audited. 
## T2 — upper-tail optimality in restricted protocol class
Define clean posterior c_k=1-v_k/w_k and F(t) as integral of top t probability mass of c, with randomized boundary atoms. F increasing concave piecewise linear. Given y, on R=0 the posterior equals c_old, on R=1 equals 0. Nonrepair atoms for y have capacities q_y w_k, total diagonal min(p_y,q_y). For any local maximal coupling and outgoing flag independent of y, the clean mass in any top t outgoing flag set of mass t is at most q_y F(min(1,t p_y/q_y)) when conditioned jointly on y (q_y=0 contributes zero). Proof: among diagonal atoms of capacities q_y w_k, highest c fill first; at most min(t p_y,min(p_y,q_y)) nonrepair mass can fit. For donor y greedy repair removes lowest c atoms and retains the top p_y/q_y fraction; for recipient y all q_y mass retained and remaining p_y-q_y repair atoms clean=0. Sorting and filling equal outgoing masses attains every rank-boundary upper bound simultaneously. Therefore F_next(t_j)=sum_y q_y F(min(1,t_j p_y/q_y)) at cumulative clean-rank flag masses t_j, interpolate linearly. F_0(t)=t; Pr(any repair)=1-F_N(1). Operator monotone under pointwise F dominance. With equal weights w=1/m, all protocols have common rank boundaries regardless of label order, so induction proves greedy maximal F at all t and minimizes union repair among fixed-order, m-state, flag-output-independent, memoryless local maximal-coupling protocols described above. For unequal weights, same proof only if rank weights fixed in that order; no global optimum across adaptive rank permutations or partitions. No claim against arbitrary interactive/parallel protocols, extra shared randomness, different draft laws, nonmaximal leaves or all-information R72. Recurrence O(N m V) for uniform grid interpolation, O(N m V log m) for nonuniform; actual local implementation can aggregate event mass by (k,R,y) in O(mV) instead of explicit O(mV^2) audit using product residual. O(m) frontier state; actual full p/q needed locally. 256 flags finite diagnostic, not continuous oracle. N-1 serial messages each ceil(log2 m) payload bits (first state can be local random); physical messages/headers/RTT cost not free. 
## T3/H — formal learning communication piggyback and strong baseline
Because final flag is independent of all committed formal output at fixed contexts/snapshot, choosing high-repair flag Z=0 (prob pi=w_0) for upload of a formal gradient G(Y,context) gives E[(1{Z=0}/pi)G|Y,context]=G. Assumes G determined solely by formal outputs, fixed private context, and any extra RNG independent of flag conditional on those; no guarantee for SD-conditioned losses, optimizer state or multi-local-step training. Conditional covariance inflation vs full is (1/pi-1)GG^T; at pi=.2, factor4. Not DP; flag may reveal repair/draft info. If both repair and update share a fixed startup b, total activation Pr(H or upload)=Pr(H)+pi-Pr(H,Z=0). Compare random independent upload at equal pi, and strong ordinary independent SD with same finite-state posthoc rank relay (local repairs uncoordinated, t_k=w_k d_x), which also makes flag independent of Y. The latter often has *lower extra upload activation* than new coupling because it has more repairs, but higher combined activation. Shared fixed startup and sufficient time/availability for gradient payload unproven; gradient must be ready before transaction closes, otherwise cannot piggyback. Same-resource flat can implement both; no training improvement established. Unbiased selection is ordinary HT; do not market as new learning optimizer.
