Retain HFL cloud/edge/client and same-backbone self-drafting. Target a workload where repeated formal rollout generation occupies an exposed critical path (federated RL/post-training is one possible application, not an assertion that supervised HFL has this bottleneck). Freeze common early-exit compatible trunk during a local interval; train bounded regional late residual and early-exit adaptation under both formal held-out quality guard and measured total time-to-quality objective; choose exit/draft length from real per-region calibration under same resource budget. Version boundary: new p only after local/region updates; within rollout fixed p, actual conditional q, standard residual verifier, no post hoc selection or deadline sample dropping. Use same independent rollout group, participation, budget, exact target contract. Compare strong HFL+official LayerSkip with matched per-region tuning, independent region adapter optimization, ordinary AR, same-delegation flat and relevant dynamic SD.
