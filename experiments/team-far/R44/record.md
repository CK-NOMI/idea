## T1. General finite-window common-proposal frontier
Fixed legitimate prefix h, E distinct fixed personalized targets p_e, same finite vocabulary V, weights a_e>=0 (survival mass). q may depend on h but not e inside a sharing group. For any policy, expected accepted length (excluding bonus) is sum over all nonempty prefixes v up to K of sum_e a_e product_j min(p_e(v_j|hv_<j), q(v_j|hv_<j)). Let F_k(h,a) be supremum of remaining weighted EAL over unrestricted common autoregressive q policies; F_0=0. Recurrence F_k=max_{q in simplex} sum_x [sum_e a_e min(p_e(x|h),q_x)+F_{k-1}(hx,(a_e min(p_e(x|h),q_x))_e)]. Nonnegative homogeneous, coordinatewise monotone, convex in a: it is the supremum of linear forms in a (policy family independent of a). For fixed h, subdivide simplex by q_x=p_e(x|h) and q_x=0,1; in each closed cell the successor mass is affine in q. The objective is convex on each cell and reaches a maximum at a cell vertex (convex combination of vertices). Thus at least one exact maximizer has all but at most one coordinate at a breakpoint; normalization determines remaining coordinate. Proof assumes finite V,K, no parameter capacity or cost restrictions; zero mass branch arbitrary. For binary V, candidates precisely 0,1 and every p_e(0|h). Enumeration exponentially expensive, requires full deep conditional probabilities, not deployable. Recovers R43 two-region two-step bound in binary tests. Not claiming DP/convex analysis techniques new. Greedy immediate overlap can be strictly suboptimal; exact optimal continuation matters.

## T2. Fixed-root, terminal-step hierarchical sharing allocation
Four fixed targets e=0..3, two physical sibling pairs {0,1},{2,3}. First proposal q_g at each edge fixed. For accepted-prefix token x define a_e(x)=w_e min(p_e(x),q_g(x)), w_e=.25. At last step, optimally sharing a single conditional proposal across siblings loses L_g(x)=min(a_i,a_j)*TV(p_i(.|hx),p_j(.|hx)) relative to separate oracle proposals, by weighted TV triangle inequality. At most one pair may split at each prefix (<=3 distinct active conditional distributions globally for that prefix). Optimal prefix-adaptive gain over two shared groups is sum_x max_g L_g(x); best fixed split gain max_g sum_x L_g(x). Strict advantage if different uniquely maximizing groups have positive mass. Counterexample all p_e(root)=(.5,.5), edge A targets opposite deterministic at x=0 but identical at x=1, edge B vice versa. EAL shared/static/adaptive/full=1.75/1.875/2/2. This is probability oracle, not hardware speedup, and assumes shared calibration prefixes/permissions. Same-information flat conditional router can reproduce exactly. Distinction from HC/R01: HiCut scores verification cut for a mixture target at fixed context; here multiple fixed personalized targets, proposal partition conditioned on earlier survival. Standard cut/conditional routing itself is not new. For arbitrary terminal tree, known weighted TV median node loss and tree knapsack with additive costs can choose a cut; not proof of runtime budget or full-window optimal tree sharing.

## H: architecture and quality contract
Cloud common backbone and global W_g; each edge formal residual R_g and shallow proposal U_g; client residual C_e with p_e=softmax((W_g+R_g+C_e)y), client shallow proposal U_e. Root uses U_g. At subsequent prefix hx choose split g or none using a common shallow regressor of [1,rms(x(h)),rms(x(hx))]; actual q_e = U_e or U_g on the selected legal prefix. No deep p available to online gate; calibrate offline on signed realized gains G_g(x)=sum_{e in g} a_e(x)[overlap(p_e(.|hx),q_e^local)-overlap(p_e(.|hx),q_g)]. Fit two ridge scores, choose positive argmax or no split. Distinct active distribution cap is imposed, not measured memory/computation; all six heads stored and trained, one projection per request either way. No joint backbone training or client/edge/cloud communication performed. Same model bank baseline fixed split selected on calibration, test best static oracle, full and no split. Gate calibration labels depend on formal targets, but SD->HFL formal learning not tested this round. Proposed constrained extension max EAL subject to each client task NLL <= reference+epsilon; use CE objective and accept/reject candidate auxiliary updates on held-out calibration or primal-dual, no true population guarantee. No claim new optimizer, standard constrained multiobjective training. Quality must be measured separately. Standard exact SD uses actual chosen q (not mixture or counterfactual) and min(1,p_e/q), residual normalized (p_e-q)_+, first-rejection truncation; for fixed p_e conditional kernel identity yields sequence law in real arithmetic, greedily verify argmax with fixed ties. Actual batch/KV/EOS/top-p/fp greedy and sampling implementation untested.
