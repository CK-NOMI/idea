## T: disjoint mass-preserving head and exact local maximal coupling
For fixed causal prefix, snapshot and disjoint sets S_g selected before candidate randomness, p_i=q_i outside union; p_i=q(S_g) softmax_{S_g}(log q_i+delta_g,i) inside. Positive q. Each group preserves mass, so donor and receiver masses match locally. Draw X~q, accept min(1,p_X/q_X), on rejection sample positive residual restricted to X's group. Marginal at i equals min(p_i,q_i)+(group donor mass)*positive residual_i/group receiver mass = p_i. Overall equality probability is 1-TV(p,q) even though residual transport is local. Outside union always accept. Conditional causal proof: independent random sources at each drafted position, accept prefix and emit first repair; discard suffix after rejection, restart at true history; no bonus necessary. Exact to new formal p only. Greedy needs global argmax (or certified upper bounds); raw top-p/temperature may break mass equality; production finite precision/EOS not audited.
Hard label y: group gradients are zero except owner group (q independent of heads and fixed masks); inside gradient softmax-e_y. Soft labels activate all groups with positive teacher mass. Shared q/backbone gradients may be dense. Disjoint head operators commute. Arbitrary nested multiplicative tilts do not commute: q=(1/3,1/3,1/3), outer S={1,2,3} weights (2,1,1), inner {1,2} weights (1,2). Outer then inner=(3/8,3/8,1/4), inner then outer=(4/11,4/11,3/11). Thus use disjoint active nodes of fixed vocabulary tree (antichain), not unrestricted nested tilts. Tree is vocabulary hierarchy, not physical HFL topology by itself.
Teacher projection per group r_i*q(S)/r(S). KL gain G(S)=sum_{i in S} r_i log(r_i/q_i)-r(S)log(r(S)/q(S)); disjoint gains additive. Expected coordinate proxy C=Σ|S| q(S), plus fixed head dispatch F per call. Train-time region clients can send weighted per-node sums of gain and cost (teacher outputs remain local) aggregated exactly; no nonlinear local optimizer equivalence claimed. For iid fixed q within B draft slots, expected head launches per candidate = [1-(1-q(S))^B]/B; only a proxy on causal blocks. Tree DP with budget K on total selected coordinates: max(skip node with child budget convolution, choose node with score G-lambda C if size<=K); O(V K^2) for binary tree after stats, exact restricted to antichain and additive surrogate. Single arbitrary support greedily initialized by best pair and expanded; stronger but not global optimizer. AR-objective and SD-objective use F=4 coordinate units and B=4, chosen exploratory, not measured. Actual target quality and end-to-end cost not known.
Direct conditional AR (same p) draws q and samples p(.|S_g) on a hit; same per-token head activation. AR can also use same random maximal coupling, same emitted path; multiple independent streams can batch. Same-delegation flat reproduces all group decisions. SD-specific group selection by proxy is a hypothesis, not necessity. A group split lowers coordinate work but reduces attainable teacher quality by mass conservation in each group. Counterexample: q=(.25,.25,.25,.25), r=(.4,.4,.1,.1), groups {1,2},{3,4}, conditional ratios within groups constant -> no improvement although whole support can exactly reproduce r. No evidence of G2 pass.

## Post-audit P: break-even and stronger causal baseline
From matched committed-path counts at budget16 λ0, let c_q denote cost of a shallow q row, c_r cost of one correction coordinate, and F fixed per-head dispatch cost (all in the same time units, linear proxy; no kernel fusion/inter-stream batching/overlap modeled). C_AR=c_q+a_r c_r+a_h F; C_SD=d c_q+s_r c_r+s_h F. Positive gain iff F(a_h-s_h)>(d-1)c_q+(s_r-a_r)c_r. To reduce this generation-only cost by 15%, require F(.85a_h-s_h)≥(d-.85)c_q+(s_r-.85a_r)c_r. These denominators positive in the two reported cells. Computed from remote raw results:
- single: dispatch reduction 67.81%, coordinate operations increase 10.22%; positive gain requires F>0.1695 c_q + 2.4115 c_r; 15% generation-only reduction requires F≥0.5383 c_q + 7.6406 c_r.
- multiSD: dispatch reduction 55.29%, coordinate operations increase 8.59%; positive gain requires F>0.1987 c_q + 1.9966 c_r; 15% generation-only reduction requires F≥0.7536 c_q + 7.5247 c_r.
For multiSD relative to single, held-out KL increases from .019106 to .019697 (3.09%), oracle KL increases from .002560 to .008276 (223.35%), per-draft expected coordinate proxy falls from 14.263 to 10.346 (27.47%). No quality-matched speed claim. A particularly strong ordinary SD baseline already uses identical group-local kernels; changing only dispatch implementation is not a distinct HFL mechanism. At λ=.001/.003 the SD cost surrogate selects different groups from AR proxy, but both quality and cost change and no superiority follows. Direct multi-stream AR can also amortize F; actual HFL training increases total denominator, making 15% overall even harder. If real measured F below break-even under strong batched AR, stop. These are cost-model inequalities, not real speed results.
