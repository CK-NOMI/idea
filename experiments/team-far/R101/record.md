## T: prefix-weighted counterfactual acceptance
For fixed start distribution and K, define a_j(h,x)=min(p_j(x|h),q_j(x|h)), m_j(x_1:t)=prod a_j. A_j=E accepted tokens=sum_{t=1}^K sum_{prefix length t} m_j. If behavior prefix law B_t>0 wherever m_j>0, estimate A_j by S_j=sum_t m_j(X_1:t)/B_t(X_1:t). Unbiased at fixed external starts, not long-run block-boundary objective (R59). Candidate independent of evaluation data or held-out selection needed. If one component is chosen at block start with probabilities pi_j>0, B_t=sum_l pi_l prod_{u<=t}q_l(x_u|h). m_j<=Q_j implies weight<=1/pi_j for every t, S_j<=K/pi_j, independent of exponential-in-K ratio bound. Uniform pi over 8 gives bound 8 per prefix. Fixed q0 example q_j(x)=p_j(x)=.9, q0(x)=.1 repeated: weight 9^t. Per-step uniform mixture b(x|h)=mean_j q_j(x|h) has worst upper M^t, not proof of actual variance disadvantage. Deterministic equally stratified draws from components with denominator mixture yield unbiased pooled MIS (standard balance heuristic). Independent random mixture sampling Hoeffding union bound M candidates: P(max_j |hatA_j-A_j|>eps)<=2M exp(-2N eps^2 pi_min^2/K^2); bound loose, candidate chosen with true feasible quality then simple regret<=2eps on event. Same holds stratified independent with expectation of pooled estimator, not individual stratum. No general guarantee over changing starts/models.
T: conditional integrate last sampled token using full evaluated probabilities: replace m_j(X_1:K)/B_K by m_j(X_1:K-1)/B_{K-1} * sum_x a_j(h,x). For iid mixture this is conditional expectation given prefix, variance of whole sum cannot increase. For fixed stratified mixture conditional law given component differs; do not claim same variance dominance, but pooled unbiased remains. Apply to all t individually gives unbiased allRB, no general whole-sum variance ordering. For AR/cached control the same estimator works. Evaluating every candidate p/q over vocabulary has real cost.
